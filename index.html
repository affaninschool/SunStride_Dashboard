ILLUSTRATIVE SIMULATION — NOT MEASURED OUTPUT. Alignment is a geometric cosine model of the angle between the panel normal and the simulated sun direction. It is not a power measurement and implies no verified efficiency gain.
How the concept works. A fixed panel keeps one orientation all day, so its angle to the sun drifts away from perpendicular. A tracking mount rotates the panel so its normal stays closer to the sun's direction.
  • Two LDR sensors separated by a small divider compare light intensity on each side.
  • A controller (Arduino Nano) reads the difference and decides which way to rotate.
  • An SG90 servo drives the panel until both sensors read nearly equally — the panel then faces the light.
  • This subsystem is separate from the ESP8266 + RC522 identification hardware.

ECOFUSION // THE ENERGY SYSTEM

UNIFIED ENERGY INTELLIGENCE

ECOFUSION

EcoFusion is the conceptual framework that explains how the four energy ideas relate — it is not a fifth energy source. Select a module to illuminate it and read its explanation.

  • The four systems rely on different physical principles and cannot be compared without consistent units and measurement conditions.
  • Each has different conversion, conditioning and storage requirements.
  • A practical integration would need suitable power electronics, protection and energy management.
  • Outputs are not summed here, because no common measurement exists.
